Configuring an AI Stack is a complex task that can include some guess work, a bit of over specing and trial and error.

Nvidia is one of the experts on GPU hardware and related software stacks.

Imagine being able to access over a hundred of their configuration recipes inside a tool. Well that's what AICR gives you.

It stands for AI Cluster Runtime, a name which is a little counterintuitive, as it's really a tool that gives you guided Cluster Configurations, which can then be deployed on a cluster with ArgoCD, Helm, Fuse or other deployment mechanisms.

It supports many different cloud service providers, gpu type, and platforms for training and inference.

The set of recipes are being constantly expanded, through both Nvidia and the community.

The purpose of this demo is to show how it is applicable to OpenShift, and how it can be improved upon.
