So let's take a look at the tool itself.

To start, let's list the recipes available. "recipe list" gives the complete set.

They are structured as bases, mixins and overlays.

Support for OpenShift OCP is through an overlay that is specifically tailored to OpenShift's Operator Lifecycle Management (OCP OLM).

So there are just 4, and they're not tailored to any specific accelerator, operating system or platform. There's a base, and one for training and two for inference.

The OCP inference NIM is a specialization of OCP inference, which is a specialization of OCP.

Others like EKS and GCP are more fine grained with options for specifying GPUs.
