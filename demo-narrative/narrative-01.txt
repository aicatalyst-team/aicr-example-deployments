Configuring an AI Stack is a complex task that can include some guess work, a bit of overspecing and trial and error.

Nvidia is one of the experts on GPU hardware and related software stacks. Imagine being able to over a hundred of their configuration recipies inside a tool. Well that's what AICR gives you.

It stands for AI Cluster Runtime, which is a little counterintuitive, as it's really a tool that give you guided Cluster Configurations which can then be deployed on a cluster with ArgoCD, Helm, Fuse or other deployment mechanisms.

It supports many different cloud service providers, gpu type, and platforms with 2 intents - training and inference.

The set of recipies are being constantly expanded, through both Nvidia and the community.

The puprpose of this demo is to show how it is applicable to OpenShift, and how it can be improved upon.
