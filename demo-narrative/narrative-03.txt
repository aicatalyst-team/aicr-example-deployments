So let's take a look at the tool itself.

The main commands to support the main workflow are all there: snapshot, recipe, bundle and verify.
Others support the management of these artifacts like query, validate, evidence, diff mirror and trust.

To start let's list the recipes available. "recipe list" gives the complete set.

They are structured as base and overlays.

Support for OCP is through an overlay that is specifically tailored to OpenShift's Operator Lifecycle Management (OCP OLM).

So there are just 4, and they're not tailored to any specific accelerator, os or platform. There's a base, and one for training and two for inference.

The ocp-inference-nim is a specialization of ocp-inference, which is a specialization of ocp.

Others like EKS and GCP are more fine grained with options for specifying GPUs.
