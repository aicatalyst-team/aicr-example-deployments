So lets have a look at the workflow:

First you might take a snapshot of your existing cluster to draw a baseline.
The recipe command allows you to use this snapshot anlong with other parameters.
In the bundle command you choose your deployment platform such as a Argo CD or Helm and a configuration is produced.

Deployment is then performed using these tools, and the validation command can use cryptographic hashes to check the deployment is running as expected and has not been tampered with.
